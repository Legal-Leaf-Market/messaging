Two-Tier Logistics System: Scheduled Shared Corridor Milk-Runs & Local On-Demand Counter Runners

Tier 1: Scheduled Shared Corridor Milk-Runs

Booked 2–3 Days Out

Maximizes revenue by density-stacking 3 to 5 regional stops into a single corridor trip. Lowers per-client cost ($50–$85 flat stop) while generating $150–$350+ revenue on ~$18 gas.

  • South I-65 Corridor: Edinburgh → Columbus → Seymour → Clarksville / River Ridge.
  • North I-65 Loop: Columbus → Edinburgh → Franklin → South Indy (Outside Processing).

Tier 2: Local On-Demand / Counter Runners

Dedicated Local Drivers

Immediate dispatch for urgent tool crib, maintenance, MRO parts counter pickups & inter-plant emergency runs within Columbus/Edinburgh.

  • Supply Counters: Applied Industrial, Kirby Risk, Motion Industries, Fastenal, Airgas, Linde.
  • Plant Cribs: NTN Driveshaft, Enkei, Rightway, Toyota Material Handling, Cummins, Hisada.
