# Private Line Courier — Lead Playbook & Route Scheduler

## Overview & Two-Tier Operating Model

Private Line Courier operates on a two-tier regional logistics model designed to maximize truck utilization, minimize fuel costs, and offer unmatched response times for industrial tool cribs and precision machine shops in South-Central Indiana.

---

### Tier 1: Scheduled Shared Corridor Milk-Runs (Booked 2–3 Days Out)

1. **South I-65 Corridor Run (Edinburgh -> Columbus -> Seymour -> Clarksville/Jeffersonville/River Ridge)**
   - **Anchor Partner:** Quick Turn Anodizing (6973 S US Hwy 31, Edinburgh, IN, (877) 716-1150).
   - **Regional Anchor Drop:** Puneet in Clarksville ($50 flat shared-truck drop for ~10 boxes).
   - **Intermediate Stops:** Jackson Developmental Industries (1820 First Ave, Seymour) & River Ridge / Clarksville machine shops.
   - **Trip Revenue Model:** 3 to 5 stacked stops at $50–$85 per stop ($150–$350+ revenue on ~$18 gas).

2. **North I-65 Outside-Processing Loop (Columbus -> Edinburgh -> Franklin -> South Indy)**
   - **Anchor Pickups:** Columbus CNC shops (Boyer Machine & Tool, Precise Tooling Solutions at 3150 Scott Dr, Cunningham Pattern, Quality Machine + Tool, Noblitt Fabricating, Falcon Mfg).
   - **Outside Processors:** Quick Turn Anodizing (Edinburgh) -> Electro-Spec Inc. (1800 Commerce Pkwy, Franklin, (317) 738-9199, plating & heat treat) & Shelbyville Plating (1010 Saint Joseph St, Shelbyville, (317) 398-7040) -> Circle City Heat Treating (2243 Massachusetts Ave, Indy, (317) 638-2252).
   - **Value Proposition:** Eliminates multi-day manufacturing delays by keeping heat treat and plating runs on predictable daily loops.

## Interactive Call Scripts & Pricing Tiers

### Pricing Structure
- **Shared Scheduled Stop:** $50 – $85 flat per stop.
- **Dedicated Hot Shot Run:** $35 base + $1.85 / mile.
- **Local Parts / Counter Run:** $35 flat per local run (<45 min delivery).

### Call Scripts
- **Corridor Pitch:** *"We run scheduled shared-corridor milk-runs along I-65 connecting machine shops directly with processors like Quick Turn Anodizing and Electro-Spec. By stacking stops, we cut your shipping costs to $50 flat per run with zero freight damage."*
- **Counter Runner Pitch:** *"When a line goes down and maintenance needs parts immediately from Kirby Risk or Applied Industrial, our dedicated local runners pick up at the counter and deliver directly to your crib in under 45 minutes."*
